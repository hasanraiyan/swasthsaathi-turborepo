## 🩺 What is Swasthya Saathi?

**Swasthya Saathi is an AI-powered personal health companion that helps a person take care of their health journey over time.**

It is **not meant to replace doctors, hospitals, or existing treatments**.

The idea is simple:

> **Your doctor treats your condition. Swasthya Saathi helps you manage and stay on top of your health journey.**

A person may already have doctors, medicines, prescriptions, lab reports, appointments, symptoms, and treatments happening outside the app. Swasthya Saathi brings that health context together and helps the user understand and manage it.

### 🤖 The AI Agent

The Agent is the intelligence inside Swasthya Saathi.

It can understand the user's health context and help with things like:

* Understanding health information
* Remembering relevant health history
* Managing medications and routines
* Understanding reports and documents
* Keeping track of treatments and doctor instructions
* Answering health-related questions
* Helping the user know what needs attention
* Following up on important health activities
* Guiding the user through their ongoing health journey

The goal isn't to make another app where someone just **enters data and looks at charts**.

The goal is to make something that feels more like:

> **“Swasthya Saathi understands my health journey and helps me take care of it.”**

And now, with the Agent, we're building the **intelligent layer that makes that possible.** 🧠💙
